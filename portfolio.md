        <h2>About Me</h2>
        <p>I didn't start in data. I started in marketing and operations at <strong>Veginova</strong>, a family-owned seed company in Odense and my employer since 2023: sourcing customers, running trade fairs, and visiting production in Costa Rica, Panama and Mexico.</p>
        <p>When a report says a variety is running short, I know which process produced that number and where it breaks, because I worked in that process.</p>
        <p style="margin-top: 28px;"><strong>What the system at Veginova covers</strong></p>
        <ul>
          <li>Excel stays the front door, with an Azure SQL database and its business rules behind it. It refreshes itself every ten minutes during the working day.</li>
          <li>Inventory and lot traceability: stock is calculated from every movement since the last count, lot by lot.</li>
          <li>Quality control: a lot can only be sold once its quality test releases it.</li>
          <li>Production planning from safety stock, expected sales, stock and incoming. The production plan matches the planner's own spreadsheet with zero mismatches.</li>
          <li>Revenue reconciles to the audited accounts on every change.</li>
          <li>Claude is set up to read photos of seed packets and bills into rows a person confirms, and nothing reaches a live system without a human check.</li>
        </ul>
        <p id="ai" style="margin-top: 28px;"><strong>How I use AI</strong></p>
        <p>I work agentic: I build with Claude Code in VS Code and GitHub, connect Claude to the database through MCP, and automate with Power Automate and Python. AI drafts from written procedures and review agents, and I review and sign off every release.</p>
        <p>In the systems I build, AI sits on top of the data foundation. The numbers come from the database, and a person confirms what the AI proposes.</p>
        <p style="margin-top: 28px;"><strong>The technical layer</strong></p>
        <p>I specialise in Power BI end to end: semantic models, DAX, Power Query, report design, distribution and access management. I connect Power BI to the sources a business already runs on: Azure SQL, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, SharePoint, Excel and APIs.</p>
        <p>Beyond dashboards: data modelling in SQL, business apps in Power Apps, and automation with Power Automate and Python. I also build on Google Workspace and on custom stacks with Postgres and Python.</p>
        <p style="margin-top: 28px;">Most of my experience is from small companies and my own, close to the decisions and owning the work end to end.</p>
        <p><strong>Certificates:</strong> Google Data Analytics · Artificial Intelligence: Implications for Business Strategy (MIT Sloan) · Power BI Fundamentals (DataCamp).</p>
        <p>I work in Danish, English and Spanish.</p>
        <p style="margin-top: 28px;"><strong>How I work on a contract</strong></p>
        <p>I work by the hour on longer engagements, inside your team or as the one person who builds the system end to end. The rate is on request and the hours are by agreement.</p>
        <p>Hybrid in Denmark up to three days a week, or remote for clients in Europe and the US.</p>
        <p>I hold the whole picture from the database to the report, so you explain your business once. I document what I build, so your own people can run and change it after the contract ends.</p>
